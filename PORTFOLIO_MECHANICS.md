# Portfolio Mechanics Explained

This document outlines how the key portfolio-related statistics are calculated and updated within the game engine.

## Key Statistics

1.  **`portfolioValue`**: The current market value of all assets held in the investment portfolio. This fluctuates due to:
    *   Market growth/decline (applied annually based on `portfolioGrowthRate`).
    *   Contributions/Investments (increases value).
    *   Liquidations/Sales (decreases value).
    *   Direct market adjustments (can increase or decrease value, simulating volatility or specific events).

2.  **`portfolioInvestedAmount`**: The cost basis of the *currently held* assets in the portfolio. This represents the sum of all contributions and direct investments *minus* the proportional cost basis of any assets that have been sold (liquidated).
    *   **Increases** when cash is contributed or directly invested (`portfolioValueChange > 0`).
    *   **Decreases** proportionally when assets are sold (`cashChange: "portfolioValueChange..."`). The decrease is calculated as `(Amount Sold / Value Before Sale) * Invested Amount Before Sale`.
    *   **Does Not Change** due to market growth/decline or direct market value adjustments (`portfolioValueChange < 0`).

3.  **`totalInvestedCapital`**: The cumulative sum of *all cash ever* put into the portfolio via contributions or direct investments. This value only increases and serves as the fundamental base for calculating overall percentage return.

4.  **`unrealizedGain`**: The difference between the current market value and the cost basis of *currently held* assets.
    *   **Calculation**: `unrealizedGain = portfolioValue - portfolioInvestedAmount`
    *   This represents the profit (or loss) you would realize *if* you sold the entire current portfolio *at this moment*.

5.  **`realizedGain`**: The cumulative total profit or loss recognized from *selling* assets.
    *   Calculated each time a liquidation occurs (`cashChange: "portfolioValueChange..."`).
    *   **Gain/Loss from Sale**: `Amount Received from Sale - Cost Basis of Assets Sold`
    *   The cost basis of assets sold is calculated proportionally based on `portfolioInvestedAmount` at the time of sale.
    *   `realizedGain` accumulates over the entire game.

6.  **`totalValueRealized`**: The cumulative total cash amount received from *all* portfolio sales (liquidations).
    *   This simply tracks the cash proceeds from sales, irrespective of gain or loss.
    *   Increases by the `liquidationAmount` during each sale event.

7.  **`totalReturnPercentage`**: The overall investment performance expressed as a percentage.
    *   **Calculation**: `((realizedGain + unrealizedGain) / totalInvestedCapital) * 100`
    *   **Numerator (`totalGain`)**: Represents the total increase in wealth generated by the portfolio activities (both realized from sales and unrealized from current holdings).
    *   **Denominator (`totalInvestedCapital`)**: Represents the total amount of money the player committed to the portfolio over time.
    *   This metric provides a comprehensive view of how effectively the player's invested capital has grown (or shrunk).

## Update Flow (`applyStatUpdate`)

The calculations happen in a specific order within the `applyStatUpdate` function to ensure accuracy:

1.  **Base Rate Updates**: Changes to `income`, `portfolioContribution` rate, and `portfolioGrowthRate` are applied first.
2.  **Age-Related Changes**: If `ageChange > 0`:
    *   Income is added to `cash`.
    *   Contributions are calculated (using the potentially updated rate) and added to `portfolioValue`, `portfolioInvestedAmount`, and `totalInvestedCapital`.
    *   Market growth is applied to `portfolioValue` (based on the updated `portfolioGrowthRate`).
3.  **Liquidation (Sale for Cash)**: If `cashChange` indicates a portfolio sale (`"portfolioValueChange..."`):
    *   The amount to liquidate is calculated.
    *   `realizedGain` is updated based on the proportional `portfolioInvestedAmount` of the sold portion.
    *   `totalValueRealized` is increased by the sale amount.
    *   `cash` is increased by the sale amount.
    *   `portfolioValue` is decreased by the sale amount.
    *   `portfolioInvestedAmount` is decreased proportionally.
4.  **Other Cash Changes**: Standard additions or subtractions to `cash` are processed.
5.  **Direct Portfolio Value Changes**: If `portfolioValueChange` is present (and *not* part of a liquidation):
    *   **Positive Change**: Treated as a direct investment. Increases `portfolioValue`, `portfolioInvestedAmount`, and `totalInvestedCapital`.
    *   **Negative Change**: Treated as a market adjustment/write-down. Decreases `portfolioValue` only. Does *not* affect `cash`, `realizedGain`, or `portfolioInvestedAmount`.
6.  **Debt & WellBeing Changes**: Applied directly.
7.  **Final Calculations**: After all primary stats are updated:
    *   `netWorth` is calculated: `cash + portfolioValue - debt`.
    *   `unrealizedGain` is calculated: `portfolioValue - portfolioInvestedAmount`.
    *   `totalReturnPercentage` is calculated using the updated `realizedGain`, `unrealizedGain`, and `totalInvestedCapital`.
8.  **Age Update**: Player's `age` is incremented.
